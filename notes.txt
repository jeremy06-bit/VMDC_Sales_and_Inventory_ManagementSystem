Cash Drawer: 
It tracks the cashier's money at the start and end of each shift.

Opening the Drawer
The cashier enters how much physical cash is in the drawer at the start of their shift.
This is recorded as opening_cash and the session status is set to open.
While open, the Open button is disabled so they can't open it twice

Closing the Shift
The cashier physically counts the cash in the drawer and enters the amount.

The system then automatically calculates:
Expected Cash = opening_cash + total sales collected that day by that cashier.
Discrepancy = physical count − expected amount

If there's any discrepancy (short or over), it shows a warning.

The session is then marked as closed

Drawer History:
Shows the last 20 sessions with: date, cashier name, opening, closing, expected, discrepancy, and status.
Only the logged-in cashier's own open session is checked for status — so each cashier manages their own drawer.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Opening Cash
No calculation — it's a manual entry
The cashier physically counts the cash in the drawer at the start of the shift and types it in
That number is saved as-is to opening_cash in the database

Closing Cash
Also no calculation — it's another manual entry
At the end of the shift, the cashier physically counts all the cash again and types it in
Saved as closing_cash

Expected Cash
This is the only one the system calculates automatically:

expected = drawer["opening_cash"] + sales

Where sales is pulled from the database:
sales = conn.execute("""
    SELECT COALESCE(SUM(total), 0) FROM sales_transactions
    WHERE date(created_at) = date('now', 'localtime')
    AND cashier_id = ?
""", (self.user["id"],)).fetchone()[0]

So in plain terms:
Expected = Opening Cash + Total Sales collected today by that cashier

Discrepancy:
discrepancy = amount - expected
Discrepancy = Closing Cash (physical count) − Expected Cash

If the result is 0 → cash matches perfectly
If negative → cash is short (money is missing)
If positive → cash is over (more money than expected)
